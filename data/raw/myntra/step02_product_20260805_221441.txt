How Should You Choose a Sofa Set by Material?

Material affects comfort, durability, and daily upkeep. Picking the right one depends on your lifestyle, climate, and budget.

Fabric Sofa Sets

Fabric offers the widest colour and pattern variety. Soft to touch, breathable in warm weather, and easy to style with cushions and throws. Sofa set prices start around Rs 15,000 for a two-seater.

Most fabric sofas clean up in under 10 minutes with a vacuum and a damp cloth. Want extra protection? Choose stain-guard treated options. A Rs 30,000 fabric sofa set costs roughly Rs 2,500 per month on a 12-month no-cost EMI.

Leather and Leatherette Sofa Sets

Genuine leather brings a premium feel and ages beautifully. Leatherette (faux leather) offers the same sleek look at half the price. Mid-range sets start at Rs 35,000. Premium leather options can reach Rs 1,50,000.

Humidity tip: In coastal or monsoon-prone regions, wipe leather surfaces weekly to prevent moisture damage. Use a quality leather conditioner every three months.

Wooden Frame Sofa Sets

Solid wood frames, especially Sheesham and Teak, last for decades. Pepperfry uses termite-treated timber for added durability. Budget Sheesham sets begin around Rs 20,000. Premium Teak options with intricate carving can exceed Rs 80,000.

Pair wooden frames with cotton cushions for better airflow during hot summers. The natural grain of each piece makes your sofa set truly one of a kind.

Which Sofa Set Size Fits Your Room?

Seating capacity must match your room dimensions. Too large, and the space feels cramped. Too small, and guests fight for seats.

1-Seater and Accent Chairs

Perfect for reading corners, bedrooms, or balconies. Average width runs 70 to 90 cm. Prices start at Rs 8,000 for basic designs. Add an ottoman for a mini lounge setup.

2-Seater Loveseats

Ideal for compact flats and studio apartments. A standard two-seater measures 140 to 160 cm wide, fitting neatly against most walls. At Rs 18,000, your monthly EMI over 12 months is just Rs 1,500.

3-Seater Family Sofas

The living room staple. Expect depths of 80 to 90 cm for proper back support. Prices range from Rs 22,000 to Rs 60,000. Most come with a 3-year frame warranty.

Sectionals and L-Shape (5 to 6 Seats)

Suit open-plan homes and large families. Modular pieces let you rearrange layouts as needed. Prices climb to Rs 50,000 and above. Assembly takes 30 to 45 minutes. Delivery teams handle setup at no extra charge.

How Do You Pick the Perfect Sofa Set for Your Room?

Follow this simple checklist before clicking "Add to Cart":

Step 1: Measure your space
Note the room length, width, and ceiling height. Leave at least 45 cm walking space around the sofa.

Step 2: Check doorways
Measure your main door, stairwell, and lift dimensions. Sectionals may need to arrive in pieces.

Step 3: Match your colour palette
Neutral walls pair well with bold sofa colours. Busy wallpaper looks best with solid upholstery.

Step 4: Test cushion firmness preference
Soft foam suits lounging. High-density foam supports posture for longer sitting.

Tip: Use Pepperfry's AR placement tool on mobile to visualise how a sofa set looks in your actual room before buying.

Frequently Asked Questions
1. What is the ideal sofa set size for a 10x12 feet room?

A 3 seater sofa set measuring 180 to 200 cm fits comfortably. Leave space for a coffee table and walking paths.

2. How do you clean a fabric sofa set?

Vacuum weekly. Blot spills immediately with a damp cloth. Use mild soap for stubborn stains and let it air dry.

3. How do you file a warranty claim?

Log in to your Pepperfry account, go to Orders, select the item, and raise a service request. Attach clear photos of the issue.

4. What is the return window for a sofa set?

Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

5. Who qualifies for no cost EMI on a sofa set?

Anyone with a credit card from partner banks can make orders above Rs 3,000. Debit card EMI is available on select banks.
