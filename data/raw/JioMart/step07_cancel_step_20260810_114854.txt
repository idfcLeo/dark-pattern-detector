You can permanently delete your account by reaching out from the contact us section on JioMart. Note: For a better experience, we recommend you NOT to delete your account if you have a. JM wallet/Gift Card balance - Make the most of the balances by shopping at JioMart b. Placed any order in the last 30 days - You will not be able to access the purchase history/returns or cancel the order if your account is deleted

However, if you delete your account if you have JM wallet/Gift Card balances or have placed any order in the 30 days, your balance amount will be forfeited and you might not be able to cancel/return the order.
