Rings

As per Roman convention, the wedding band is worn on the left-hand ring finger on the grounds that there was thought to be a vein in the finger, insinuated as the 'Vena Amoris' or the 'Vein of Love' said to be specifically associated with the heart. When it comes to jewellery, most people prefer to gift a ring for loved ones.

At BlueStone every piece has a story, as it belongs to a particular collection which is theme or inspiration based. We have our own in-house design team consisting of world class designers giving life to each design. These products are then individually cast and handcrafted to perfection. We have thousands of exclusive designs catering to every need of our customers – from dailywear jewellery and officewear jewellery to jewellery for special occasions.    

At BlueStone we offer different styles of rings

Cocktail Rings

Cocktail rings first became stylish in the 1930s and became prominent through the 1940s and 1950s. Cocktail Rings are always designed with bigger Stone or Motif in the center and surrounded by smaller diamonds or colour stones. It’s a standalone piece, as one can simply wear the single big “Ring” as the only jewellery since cocktail rings are supposed to be eye-catching. These are best suitable for Indo-western and western attire and for parties or any such special occasion. This is a perfect gift for anniversaries, Valentine day and other such similar special occasions. One of the best pieces in this collection is “THE SAZERAC RING” .

Engagement Rings

Egyptians are credited with having imagined the wedding band and the old Greeks with having embraced the custom. Since “Engagement” ceremony is one of the most memorable moments in one’s life, diamonds are the traditional choice for engagement rings. They are classic, timeless, durable as well as lasting. Solitaire engagement rings continue to be the most popular choice for engagement rings around the world. They are perfect for women who appreciate classic style and want to make a bold statement. One of the best pieces in the engagement collection is Toi et Moi ring.

Couple Bands

Couple Bands are designed in the same patterns for both men and women. No doubt, it reflects the couple sentiment; "Made for each other", as two different bands with similar design and one outline; indicating the concept of two bodies with one soul.  It’s usually worn on the occasions of Engagement, Wedding or as the perfect gift during Anniversary and Valentine’s Day. Usually, couple band rings are customised with engravings of engagement or wedding anniversary dates, or with names and memorable love notes. They are good for regular wear. One of the most popular designs in the Couple Bands collection is “THE DUAL SONATA RING FOR HER” and “THE DUAL SONATA RING FOR HIM”.

Religious Rings

We at BlueStone offer a wide range of rings with religious motifs.  One of our popular collection listed under Religious rings is the Navratna Collection. Navaratna is a Sanskrit compound word meaning “nine gems”. As indicated by Hindu astrology, an Individual’s life on earth is impacted by the nine planets or 'Navagrahas'. Every stone is set as per the arrangement of Navagrahas; for example, Ruby is placed at the center representing the sun. Hence it’s believed that wearing these nine gemstones will have a positive impact over an individual’s life. One of the best pieces in the Navratna collection is “ The Naveen Darpan Ring”

Eternity Rings

An eternity ring is an eternity band with a “nonstop” line of same cut gemstones or diamonds. It’s a design which represents the bond of “never-ending love”. These are usually given by a husband to his wife on the occasion of an anniversary or any special occasion. These designs are very classic and will never get outdated. These go best with Indo-western and western outfits.  One of the best pieces of the Eternity Collection is “THE LYNSA RING”

Cluster Rings

Cluster Rings come with unique designs consisting of smaller diamonds and combination of diamond and coloured stone. A diamond cluster ring is a physical symbol of your lifetime commitment to your spouse. After all, we all know that a diamond is forever! Cluster ring means that you give each other the future, no matter what it holds. The majority of people today wish to buy ring designs online, customised to their specifications. At BlueStone we have the widest range of rings; ideally suited for daily wear, office wear or for any special occasions. One of the best pieces in the Cluster Ring collection is “THE VENUS RING”

Customers today prefer to buy rings online due to wide choice of exclusive ring designs and competitive online ring prices.
