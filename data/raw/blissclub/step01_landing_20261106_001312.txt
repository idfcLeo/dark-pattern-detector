It started with a simple truth: the activewear I needed didn’t exist. What began as a personal mission turned into something bigger—Blissclub. From day one, the goal was clear: make movement joyful, inclusive, and accessible for Indian women.

Welcome to a club where we don’t settle, we take up space, and we always, always #KeepMoving.

This one’s for you.
